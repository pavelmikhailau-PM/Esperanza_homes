Planning assumptions

The plan rests on these assumptions. If one of them does not hold, the dates that depend on it move (SOW, section 7).

Dates and inputs

  • Kickoff on Wednesday, October 14, 2026; the setup window runs five business days (Oct 14–20) and the build starts October 21.
  • Habitat's design is approved part by part, each part before work on it starts (SOW): header and menus first, by November 6, and the last part by November 20, 2026. These approvals are the main gate in the plan.
  • Start items from Rhodes arrive within the setup window. If one arrives later, the build start and the launch date move by the same number of days.

Team and reviews

  • Up to two engineers at a time depending on the stage; the Tech Lead about one day a week.
  • The plan allows five business days to review each deliverable; the Agreement allows up to ten. If a review takes the full period, the dates that depend on it move.

Holidays and warranty

  • No deliveries between December 24 and January 1 (Christmas and New Year in the US and for the FocusReactive team), and a short week for Thanksgiving, November 26–27.
  • The project ends when the warranty period under Section 21 of the Agreement ends: 30 days from production launch.

How we run the project

Communication

  • Weekly call with Rhodes: progress, blockers, hours used, and inputs needed in the next two weeks.
  • A project Slack channel for day-to-day questions; Trello for the backlog of 27 deliverables.
  • A written status report after each call.

Delivery and acceptance

  • Two business days' notice before each delivery, delivered on the preview site.
  • Acceptance only against each deliverable's "Accepted when" checks; one consolidated list of findings from the reviewer per deliverable.
  • Any scope change goes through an approved change estimate before work starts.
